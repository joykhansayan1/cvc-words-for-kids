CVC Words for Kids: Read helps children ages 4–8 learn to read by sounding out and blending short consonant–vowel–consonant words such as cat, dog and sun. It works fully offline, with tap-to-hear audio, picture matching, build-a-word and letter tracing. No ads, no data collected.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all the lessons?

The app is free to try. Tap “Unlock Everything” on the welcome screen, answer the grown-up question, and complete the one-time purchase. It’s a lifetime unlock — pay once, keep forever. No subscription.

I already paid on another device. How do I restore?

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) → Restore Purchases, or use “Restore Purchases” on the unlock screen. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Why is there a math question before buying?

Apple’s Kids Category requires a parental gate before any purchase or link that leaves the app. It keeps children from making purchases without a grown-up.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Everything — words, pictures, audio and activities — is built into the app and works 100% offline. Internet is only used by Apple to process the one-time purchase.

Do you collect any data about my child?

No. We collect nothing. See our Privacy Policy.
