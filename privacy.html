Privacy Policy

CVC Words for Kids: Read · Effective 17 July 2026

CVC Words for Kids: Read does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app is designed for children ages 4–8 and is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Apple Kids Category requirements, and GDPR-K.

What we collect

Nothing. The app works entirely offline. It has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Learning progress

A small amount of learning progress (which words have been opened) is saved only on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage. It never leaves the device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Purchases

The app offers a single one-time “Lifetime Unlock” in-app purchase. All payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment information. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Children’s privacy

This app is made for young children. Because we collect no data whatsoever, no personal information about a child is ever gathered, used, or disclosed. All commerce and any external links are placed behind a parental gate so that only an adult can access them.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions? Email dmjks120@gmail.com.
